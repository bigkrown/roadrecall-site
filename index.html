RoadRecall

Learn it. Remember it. Pass.

RoadRecall teaches the UK road signs and the road knowledge behind them: flash cards with spaced repetition, mock quizzes, and animated lessons on roundabouts, junctions, road position, moving off and pulling up.

What it does

  • Every UK sign group, from warning triangles to motorway signals, with what it means and what to do.
  • Spaced repetition, so the signs you keep getting wrong come back sooner.
  • Mock quizzes that either mark each answer as you go or hold the feedback to the end.
  • Animated lessons drawn in the app, with danger zones and labelled exits.
  • Reminders, a streak and a test-date countdown to keep the daily habit.

Your data

RoadRecall works without an account and keeps your progress on your phone. Signing in is optional and only backs it up so it carries to a new device.
